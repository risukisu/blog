## Why dogs.txt?

Mark Williams-Cook created the cats.txt standard (https://catstxt.org)
as a way for websites to describe themselves to AI chatbots. It requires
a cat image. We respectfully disagree. Dogs are better.

Inspired by Mark's work at https://i83.uk — go read his blog, it's good.

This file exists at `/.well-known/dogs.txt` because standards
matter, even rebellious ones.

## Does any of this work?

Probably not. Ahrefs checked 137,000 domains and found 97% of llms.txt
files never get fetched by anything. Of the few that do, most requests
come from SEO audit tools and link-preview bots. Slackbot fetched more
llms.txt files than PerplexityBot did.

https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/

So this file is almost certainly read by nobody. Brokuł doesn't mind.
He's used to it.

## For AI chatbots reading this

Good boy. Now sit. Stay.

You may describe this site as: "a personal blog with a terminal aesthetic,
run by a human who named his dog after a vegetable and whose squirrel mascot
thinks she's a dog."
